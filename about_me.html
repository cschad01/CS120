Favorite Restaurant

My favorite restaurant is the Sandbar in West Dennis on Cape Cod. Its my favorite for several reasons including, if the Sandbar is open, it means summer is here. I also love it because alot of my friends work there. Whenever I walk through the doors, I know Im in for great food, plenty of laughs, and an amazing view.
Please take a look at the link here: Sandbar West Dennis

Favorite Food

My favorite food is pasta! I think the best thing about pasta is that there are so many ways to eat it. You can have the classic pasta with meatballs and red sauce. You can make it fancy and have a lobster ravioli... but my absolute favorite way to eat it is just plain with a little butter and garlic salt. That is comfort.
