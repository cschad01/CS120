The Cape Cod Slackers

Bringing the Party to Cape Cod!

The Cape Cod Slackers have become a local favorite by performing a wide variety of music that appeals to audiences of all ages. From classic rock and country favorites to modern hits, Hollywood and Mark Hennessy know how to keep the crowd singing, dancing, and having a great time. Their energetic performances and strong connection with the audience have made them one of Cape Cod's most popular live acts.
